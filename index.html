    Everything you need to manage your stock

    Ventra has two parts that work together: a mobile app for the people handling stock, and a web dashboard for the people managing it.

    Mobile app

    For your warehouse and store team

    • Scan barcodesPoint the camera at a barcode to find a product or update its stock.
    • Stock in and outAdd, remove or mark items damaged, with a reason for each change.
    • Low-stock alertsSee what's running low or out, and restock in one tap.
    • Exact locationsSection, shelf and slot for every product.
    • Barcode labelsNo barcode? Ventra creates one, and you print the label from the app.
    • Product requestsManagers ask for new products to be added to the catalogue.

    Web dashboard

    For managers and admins

    • Every branch in one placeSwitch between branches, or see all of them together.
    • Reports and PDF exportDaily and weekly movements, stock by category and low-stock lists.
    • Approve requestsAccept or reject the products your managers ask for.
    • Staff and rolesAdd people with a 4-digit code and decide who can do what.
    • Audit logEvery change, with who made it and when.
    • Your brandingYour name, logo and colours on the app and the dashboard.
